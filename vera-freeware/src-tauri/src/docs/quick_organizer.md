# Quick Organizer

## What this module does
Quick Organizer is a private, local task manager with AI assistance. Create and manage tasks across three lists — Today, This Week, and Someday — and ask VERA to help you prioritize or break down your work. Everything stays on your device.

## Quick start
1. Click "Quick Organizer" in the sidebar
2. Type a task in the input field and press Enter or click Add
3. Use the Today / This Week / Someday tabs to organize your tasks
4. Click the circle next to a task to mark it complete
5. Click "Ask VERA" for AI-powered planning assistance

## Common tasks

### Adding a task
Type in the input field at the top of the list and press Enter. Tasks are added to whichever list tab is currently active.

### Moving a task between lists
Click the "..." menu on any task and choose Move to Today, Move to This Week, or Move to Someday.

### Asking VERA to prioritize your day
Click "Ask VERA" → "Prioritize Today" to get a recommendation on which tasks to focus on first based on what you have listed.

### Breaking a task into steps
Click "Ask VERA" → select a task → "Break this down" to get 3-5 concrete first steps for any task you're not sure how to start.

### Estimating how long something will take
Click "Ask VERA" → select a task → "How long will this take?" for a realistic time estimate with brief reasoning.

### Adding notes to a task
Click on any task title to expand it and add free-form notes.

## What Quick Organizer cannot do
- Sync tasks across devices
- Set reminders or calendar events
- Track expenses or receipts (available in Business Organizer Pro)
- Find government grants or programs (available in TaxMate Pro)
- Recurring tasks or deadlines
- Share tasks with others
